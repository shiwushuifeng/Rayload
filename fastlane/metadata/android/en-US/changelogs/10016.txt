1.0.4

First F-Droid release.

- Swipe gestures on a real device: left deletes, right pauses, resumes or shares.
- Advanced checksums: MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512, BLAKE3, SM3 and CRC32 in one
  pass, with a local cache, export and published-digest lookup.
- Plugin import repaired end to end, including the Android file picker.
- One colour per device across settings, task cards and the detail page.
- Responsive settings tabs on phones and two columns on tablets.
- Storage tools: clear the download cache and analyse storage usage.
- Finished APKs install and finished media preview in one tap.
